Hi, I'm Nonhlanhla Kunene!

Founder & Candle Maker of ScentedCo

I'm a 21-year-old entrepreneur based in Cape Town, South Africa with a passion for creating good-smelling, hand-poured candles that transform any space into a sanctuary by just inhaling the atmosphere.

Why I started Scented.co

It all started in our kitchen at home during a quiet Sunday afternoon. I lit a candle and realised how much a single scent could change your mood, your energy, your entire space. From that moment I knew I wanted to share that feeling with others. Scented.co was born out of love, creativity and a dream to bring luxury into everyday moments.
